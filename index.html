01 / FINAL COURSE PROJECT · 3D · WORLD-BUILDING

Odyssey Ship

BRIEFING / 01

Developed as a final Professional Aptitude Project, Odyssey Ship reinterprets Ulysses’ departure vessel in the year 3050, a future dominated by robots, long after humans have disappeared.

BRIEFING / 02

The vessel was conceived not only as a visual object, but as something that could be assembled plausibly. Its scale suggests orbital construction, a modular logic comparable in principle to the way the International Space Station was assembled in orbit.

PRODUCT FILM · 3D

Kuru Toga

The study asks a simple question. What happens when the language of a perfume commercial is applied to an ordinary mechanical pencil?

Research, modelling, lighting, animation and editing were used to elevate an everyday object through rhythm, restraint and detail.

03 / AUTOMOTIVE PHOTOGRAPHY

03 / AUTOMOTIVE PHOTOGRAPHY

Porsche 718 Cayman

A self-directed photographic study of proportion, material and the emotional charge of warm morning light.

Independent photographic study. Not commissioned by or affiliated with Porsche.

PHOTOGRAPHY / OBJECT / LIGHT

Automotive photography is approached here as an exercise in observation.

Proportion, surface, material and light become a way of reading the object beyond its function.

The intention is not simply to document a car, but to understand how engineering, atmosphere and visual culture can coexist within a single image.

Selected commissions are developed privately, in dialogue with the object, its context and the image it requires.

PROFILE / 01

Filipe Rodrigues

JUNIOR 3D ARTIST · IMAGE-MAKER

Filipe Rodrigues is a Portuguese junior 3D artist and image-maker based near Lisbon. His work moves between 3D visualisation, photography, moving image and designed objects, combining technical precision with atmosphere and visual storytelling.

Drawn to objects, transport, light and spatial composition, his work is built through observation, experimentation and detail.
