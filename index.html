01 / Product film

Independent academic concept · 2026

An everyday tool.
Given the luxury treatment.

A mechanical pencil rebuilt through technical drawing, accurate modelling and the visual language of fragrance advertising.

Treat the familiar

The Uni Kuru Toga was studied from multiple angles, broken into technical parts and modelled in Blender before lighting, animation and editing transformed it into a monochrome product campaign.

02 / Flagship project

Concept design · 3D visualisation · 2026

A 3,000-year-old journey.
Rebuilt for 3050.

Homer's departure vessel reimagined as a 380-metre atmospheric and suborbital ship for a robot-dominated future.

Odyssey Ship / technical sequence

A vertical reading of the project: concept, exterior, interior, light and atmosphere. Fewer interruptions, more focus.

03 / Interior

Docking hangar

Operational circulation, guide lighting and reflective surfaces organise the internal threshold of the ship.

Lighting study

Form after daylight.

The night view isolates navigation lights, landing geometry and the ship’s underside structure.

The first sketch was loose.
The final system was not.

The project moves from narrative research and silhouette studies into precise modelling, material development and cinematic image-making. A six-level interior organises docking, logistics, operations, command and technical maintenance around a central vertical circulation system.

Scale
380 metres
Interior
Six levels
Primary tools
Rhino + Blender
Role
Concept to final image

Process note: the ship and base environments were designed and modelled in 3D; selected final images use AI-assisted post-production for atmosphere.

A name, a practice,
an archive.

Filipe Rodrigues is a Portuguese junior 3D artist and image-maker based near Lisbon. His practice moves between product visualisation, imagined environments, photography and moving image.

Drawn to objects, transport and architecture, he studies the point at which technical precision begins to create atmosphere, presence and desire. This evolving archive documents finished work, experiments and the thinking behind a long-term independent practice.
